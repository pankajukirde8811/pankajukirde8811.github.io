01 · About

Instrumentation roots, embedded focus

Hands-on with the whole chain: sensor, board, firmware, enclosure and screen.

I am an Instrumentation and Embedded Systems engineer pursuing an M.Sc. in Electrical and Embedded Systems at Hochschule Ravensburg-Weingarten (Ravensburg-Weingarten University of Applied Sciences, RWU).

My path runs from a Diploma and B.E. in Instrumentation Technology in Mumbai to industry work as a Design Engineer, where I built an HMI for a hydrogen-based solar induction system at IIT Bombay, debugged RTD temperature controllers, laid out PCBs and prepared FreeCAD flat patterns.

I also like teaching: I have written workshop content and taught school students to code robots with Blockly and MicroPython.

I am looking for a Werkstudent or internship in embedded design, sensor interfacing, automation, PCB design or hardware development in Germany.
